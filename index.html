Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 • Last Updated: January 1, 2025

1. Introduction & Commitment

DocumentReader (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides an integrated document and web reader software application for mobile and desktop systems. We believe your reading materials, documents, and privacy are fundamentally personal. This Privacy Policy details our transparent policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of data when you use DocumentReader on Android (via Google Play Store), Windows, macOS, or Linux.

2. Device Permissions & Local Storage Usage

In compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Android Privacy Policies, DocumentReader requires minimal runtime permissions to fulfill core reading operations:

  • Storage / Document Media Permissions (READ_MEDIA_DOCUMENTS / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used solely to read files explicitly selected by you through the system file picker. DocumentReader renders all PDFs, ePUBs, and text files locally on your physical device. We NEVER scan your broader file system, and local file contents are NEVER transmitted or copied to our servers.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively for two user-directed operations: (1) fetching HTML text when you input an online article URL to use Reader Mode, and (2) communicating with our secure backend to sync reading progress and bookmarks.

3. Phone Number & OTP Authentication Data

To provide cross-device synchronization without tracking passwords or social identity graphs, DocumentReader uses a telephone number login mechanism. Your phone number is collected solely to send a One-Time Password (OTP) verification code and to create an anonymized cryptographic account identifier (uid). We do NOT share, rent, sell, or disclose your telephone number to advertising networks or third-party brokers.

4. Third-Party Services & Crash Reporting

DocumentReader employs standard Android infrastructure provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services: Used for secure application distribution, automated license verification, and in-app updates.
  • Anonymized Crash Diagnostics: In the event of an application crash, diagnostic stack traces (devoid of document contents, file paths, or personal user data) may be sent to identify memory leaks or PDF rendering faults.

5. Data Retention, GDPR, and Children's Privacy

We adhere strictly to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). DocumentReader does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. Synchronized cloud tokens, bookmarks, and URLs are retained only as long as your account remains active. You may purge all your data instantly using the interactive tool below.

6. Privacy Inquiries & Data Protection Officer

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding DocumentReader's data processing or this policy, please contact our data safety team directly at: privacy@documentreader.app.
